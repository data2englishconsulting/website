About Me

I used to be a teacher, and my expertise was taking difficult math and science topics and distilling them to make the information accessible to everyone. Then I got a PhD in Chemistry, and I have spent the past 10 years taking real problems, like bad air quality or trying to manage Parkinson's disease symptoms or even determining long term profitability of a company, and translating it into questions that can be answered by data, and then translating the answers into actionable insights.

I have worked at a data consultancy for 5 years creating algorithms for medical devices, and to date, I have shipped I have shipped 10+ projects, typically in teams of 1-5, from a non-technical business vision through experimental design through model creation and delivery. I have owned these projects from conception to delivery, and clients love that I am not only technical, but that I am accessible (that's my teaching coming into play).

I value ethics, honesty, and simplicity. I am here to help solve your problems; I don't waste time giving you a complicated solution to justify a higher salary. I would love to chat to learn more about how I can help improve your company's data!
